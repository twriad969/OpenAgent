# Emotional Design Notes (Applied)

This project’s UI update is based on emotional design principles:

1. **Visceral level** — first impression and visual warmth.
2. **Behavioral level** — smooth interactions, clear feedback, low friction.
3. **Reflective level** — trust, confidence, and sense of progress while building.

Applied patterns in LandingForge:

- Calm gradients and soft rounded surfaces to reduce cognitive pressure.
- Motion with purpose only (`softRise`, pulse indicator) to communicate state changes.
- Human microcopy (“Crafting your preview…”, “What should the website feel like next?”).
- Clear progress framing with streaming event feed and live preview.
